
# ✪ Circle Moves:

- Bang;
    

- From a circle, keyhole in and then point directly away from the middle of your circle (expand), on the second go adjust in the same direction you did your keyhole on a larger circle
    

- Big Bear;
    

- Ride a Half Moon from E or B to X, then a straight line back to the corner letter on the same wall (a half peak)
    

- Big Cub;
    

- Ride a straight line from a corner letter to X, then a Half Moon from X to E or B (back towards the same long wall)
    

- Collapse;
    

- From a circle, on the first go cut towards the middle of your circle (shrink). On the second go, curl over in the same direction to face directly away from the middle of your circle (expand). On the third go, cut back onto your original circle size, going the same direction for each move
    

- Crown;
    

- In a rotary, the inside line will Bloom, and the outside line will Cave on the same GO
    

- Curve;
    

- Dodge away from the center of the circle to expand it
    

- Daisy;
    

- 1st GO, Expand, 2nd GO, Curl Over right and aim towards the center, 3rd GO, reform circle going the same way as before
    

- Divide Circle;
    

- Ones will lead into a circle in the direction called. Twos will lead into a circle in the opposite direction
    

- Double Sunflower;
    

- Odds galaxy cave, Evens Q-Curl fate bloom
    

- Drain;
    

- In rotary, the inner circle will expand to merge with the outer circle on the 1st go. On the second go the original outer circle will shrink to become the inner circle
    

- Eclipse;
    

- A Quarter Moon, followed by a Half Moon the opposite direction, followed by another Quarter Moon (taking up the entire width of the arena)
    

- Firework;
    

- Fate in or out in a circle
    

- Flare;
    

- 1st go: Keyhole cave, 2nd go: Curl over right, 3rd go: Merge circle
    

- Frog;
    

- Odds curl over to the outside of the circle resulting in a rotary
    

- A Half Circle;
    

- Ride half of a circle in the specified size/direction, then ride straight to the point where the half circle was started, turning in the direction called at the wall/point you started at
    

- A Horse shoe;
    

- From one line or split lines, split out onto the specified circle size, passing boots brushing. Then, either merge back into one line or continue in a tier going the opposite direction, the commander will specify during the shape
    

- ⚠ Jojo;
    

- Split lines coming towards each other circle around each other in the specified size, passing riders in the other line boot to boot, left to left. Then, cut mid merge at the starting point of the circle into the circle
    

- Lilly Pad;
    

- After dye divide on go the lines will curve around to form a rotary
    

- Petal;
    

- On go, from a circle, designated riders gradually curve out of a circle onto a designated circle size larger and going the opposite direction
    

- Pop;
    

- 1st go: Keyhole bloom, 2nd go: Merge Circle
    

- Portion;
    

- Ride the indicated amount of the specified circle size, then exit straight out of the circle (ex: 5m ½ Portion Right around X)
    

- ¼ Portion - Enter at a circle point, ride to the next circle point and exit (a quarter of the circle)
    
- ½ Portion - Enter at a circle point, ride 2 circles points and exit (half of the circle)
    
- ¾ Portion - Enter at a circle point, ride 3 circles point and exit (three-fourths of a circle)
    

- Primrose;
    

- Odds canter bloom, events walk cave
    

- Rotary;
    

-  two concentric circles moving in opposite directions
    

- Spark;
    

- Thread in or out
    

- Sparkler;
    

- Twist in or out within a circle
    

- Spike;
    

- 1st go: Expand, 2nd go: Retreat curl right, 3rd go: Merge
    

- Spiral;
    

- From a circle, start curving in by half a quadrant each quarter of your circle (ex: 20, 17.5, 15, 12.5, 10, 7.5, 5, 2.5) until you are curling around X, then on the 2nd go do the opposite, expanding your circle by half a quadrant each quarter
    

- Sunflower;
    

- Odds walk cave, evens Canter bloom
    

- Tulip;
    

- Odds will bee curl bloom while the evens I-curl cave

# Flat Moves:

- A Fan Halt;
    

- When called, the leader of the line will walk 5 steps, then halt. All others walk to their spot in their labeled direction, each rider halting at an increased slanted angle, horse tail’s touching, creating a semi circle shape
    

- NTT Jagged;
    

- When called, the leader of the line will walk 3-5 steps, then halt. All others walk to their spot. The second and 3rd riders will arrow halt with the lead and 4th and 5th riders will flock the second and 3rd riders, being in line with the lead. The pattern will repeat down the line
    

- Zebra Halt;
    

- When called, the leader of the line will walk 5 steps, then halt. All others walk to their spot in the direction asked, halting side by side on a slanted angle in the direction asked (a slanted NTT Flat)
    

- Colony;
    

- Walk on pre, on GO, odds halt on a slant going left, evens halt on a slant going right

# Gaps:

- Mirror Curls;
    

- Do the stated curl normally, then in reverse
    

- Reverse curls;
    

- Do the stated curl in reverse.
    

- Fate Curls;
    

- Riders will fate for each transition move (bee curls, etc), but you’d do one curl one way, then the other, the opposite way. (e.g ‘’Bee Fate Left’’ > Trot curl left, canter curl right).
    

- Triad;
    

- Ride the curl said on go, and then the first curl gait as the 3rd curl of the triple curl. For example - an Orbit Triad would be a walk curl, canter curl, walk curl
