# ✪ Circle Moves:

- Bloom;
    

- *Curl away from the center* of the circle
    

- Cave;
    

- *Curl towards the center* of the circle
    

- Circle;
    

- Follow your leader as you form a circle
    

- Expand;
    

- *Comb away from the center* of the circle on 1st GO, then cut back into the circle towards your lead on 2nd GO. (*Changes circle size*)
    

- Flower;
    

- A fate in or out of the circle
    

- Frog;
    

- Odds or Evens *curl over in a circle to form a rotary*. Can be done in or out, and also be done in secondary labels
    

- Full Moon;
    

- Ride half of a 20m circle, then ride straight
    

- Half Moon;
    

- Half of a 10m circle, then ride straight
    

-  Knot;
    

- In a circle, odds keyhole out, cross through the center of the circle, then merge back into their original spot in the circle
    

- Pass Cut;
    

- With all riders in a circle, designated leaders in the split lines that made the circle (or the lead and the rider across from the lead) will cut on GO *towards the middle of the circle, passing left to left* and turning in the *opposite direction once they reach the other side of the circle to switch sides- this changes the direction of the circle*
    

- Quarter Moon;
    

- Half of a 5m circle, then ride straight
    

- Rotary;
    

- A circle inside of a circle, one line going clockwise, the other going counterclockwise (Typically 5m and 10m)
    

- Shrink;
    

- In a circle, on the first go, everyone cuts towards the center, and on the second go merges back into the circle. The *circle will be smaller after this move is completed*

# Flat Moves:

- Arrow; 
    

- Riders halt with the horse’s front legs lined up with the person next to them’s back legs. Lead is the tip of the arrow. *L-R pattern*
    

- ⚠ Bow Wave;
    

- 1 member at both endpoints will bow then the pattern continues
    

- Domino;
    

- All riders will walk on 1st GO, Lead will stop on 2nd GO (or at marker), and all other riders drift in the direction called with *back hooves aligned with the person beside them’s front hooves*
    

- Flock;
    

- Riders halt with back legs lined up with the person next to them’s front legs. *Lead is the bottom of the V-shape*. *L-R pattern*
    

- Halt, pre, go;
    

- All riders will walk on PRE, once GO is called, the lead will stop and all other riders will compress NTT to halt
    

- NTT Flat;
    

- Horses standing side by side with boots *brushing each other, not crushing*
    

- Rear Halt;
    

- From the halt, press your backwards key, then your X key quickly. Or, from the walk/trot/canter, press X
    

- Hard Stop; 
    

- Once called all riders stop where they are, can use GO 
    

- Pivots
    

- ¼, ½, ¾, full pivots
    

- FLAW
    

- Follow Leader At Wall

# Gaps:

- BOS Gaps;
    

- ‘Back Of Saddle’ gaps, keep your *horse’s nose touching the back of the saddle* of the rider in front of you
    

- NTT Gaps;
    

- *Nose-to-tail*, Horse’s nose is almost touching the tail bow of the person in front of you, *not touching, but very close*
    

-  Slant Alignment;
    

- After a slant set, ensure your front hooves and back hooves align with the horse in front of you (side by side)
    

-  Splitting/ Merging
    

- When splitting at a wall or a specified point, odds will turn left and evens will turn right with all odds following their odds lead, and all evens following their evens lead (second rider in line) in OH gaps. When merging, the line rejoins together (odds always merge first, regardless of previous order- automatically relabel) at a specified point
    

- Waterfall Alignment;
    

- On your screen, you should be aligned with the person across from you
    

- Pick Up;
    

- On the first go, odds trot. On the second go, evens trot (called so that line is in comb alignment when all riders are trotting)
